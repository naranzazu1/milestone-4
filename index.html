Where the Western Diamondback Appears

The Western Diamondback Rattlesnake (Crotalus atrox) leaves a strong footprint in biodiversity databases. This plot shows the number of coordinate-bearing GBIF occurrence records by observation year, split by country.

Read the plot carefully

The recent rise reflects more records being collected and shared, not necessarily more snakes. GBIF occurrence records are observations contributed by museums, researchers, community science projects, and other data publishers. They are useful for seeing where the species has been recorded, but they are not a complete census.

The plot uses GBIF taxon key 2444356 and filters to records with coordinates. View the live GBIF taxon search and the GBIF occurrence search for the underlying records.
